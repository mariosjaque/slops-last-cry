Keyboard Controls

The story changes depending on the choices you make.

Click / Space / Enter / ↓ scroll the text and, at the end of a page, turn to the next one. The mouse wheel also scrolls.

1 to 4 pick a choice. ←, Backspace, a right click or the Back button go back one page.

Esc or the Menu button open the Table of Contents (jump to the start of a chapter, save, settings). F toggles fullscreen.

Progress is saved automatically on every page. To load, use "From saved location" on the title screen. Choices you have already taken are shown in grey.
